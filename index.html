SEOUL — OCTOBER 2026

An Unfamiliar
Kind of Beautiful.

NMIXX's sixth mini album Strange Muse releases October 19, 2026, led by title track "Birthday Wish" (literally translated as "Long Wish") — a dreamy comeback JYP Entertainment is pitching as carrying the group's momentum into "another autumn." Of the EP's six tracks, three are B-side duets pairing different members together. It follows April's pre-release single "Crescendo" and May's self-titled EP Heavy Serenade, and lands just weeks before the group closes out its first world tour, EPISODE 1: ZERO FRONTIER, with a three-night encore back where it started — Incheon's INSPIRE Arena. This page is a working build of how NMIXX Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking NSWER — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on the lineup: NMIXX is a six-member group — Lily, Haewon, Sullyoon, Bae, Jiwoo, and Kyujin. Founding member Jinni left the group in December 2022; NMIXX has promoted as a six-member act ever since, and this page treats the current six as the group, full stop.

SEOUL — OCT 16

NMIXX gets six donuts, one per member, for the Strange Muse launch

To mark its sixth mini album, NMIXX has teamed up with the dessert brand Landis Donut on a limited run lasting through November 30. Six shops across Seoul, Jeju, Busan, Daegu and Suwon sell donuts built from each member's suggestions, and the Yeonnam branch is dressed as the donut shop from the group's "O.O" video. Eight-piece sets come with a photocard set, while fans who post about their purchase can enter a raffle for signed CDs.

INCHEON — AUG 19

NMIXX to close first world tour with a three-night Incheon encore in November

NMIXX will cap off its debut world tour, EPISODE 1: ZERO FRONTIER, with three encore shows at Incheon's INSPIRE Arena on November 27, 28, and 29 — the same venue where the tour opened with two sold-out shows in late November 2025. Across 21 shows in 17 cities spanning Europe, North America, and Asia, the European leg marked the group's first stand-alone concerts in the region, and all six North American dates sold out. Ticket details for the Incheon finale were not yet announced as of this report, with the group's official channels expected to confirm sales later.

Summarized from Sports Kyunghyang and Korea JoongAng Daily by NMIXX Wire staff.

7.5 / 10THE BIAS LIST
"Even with all its twists and turns and moments of experimental surprise, Crescendo doesn't do much for me."

"Crescendo" (2026): the pre-release single NMIXX dropped ahead of their current album cycle. The Bias List gave it a mixed Grade C (7.5/10 — Hooks 7, Production 8, Longevity 8, Bias 7), praising the verses' structural twists and tempo shifts while arguing the chanted, Western-pop-leaning chorus stalls the song's momentum rather than building on it. The review frames "Crescendo" less as a chart bid and more as a pre-release buzz track — a mood-setter ahead of the fuller statement NMIXX was building toward.

It's a useful marker of where the group's experimental streak was heading right before Strange Muse: a record built, per its own teaser campaign, around deliberate mystery rather than an obvious hook.
